Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Verdant.

Shopping & Products

How do I shop for products on Verdant?

Visit our Shop page to explore available product categories. You can browse products, search for specific items, and use the available filters to find what you need.

How do I add products to my basket?

Find the product you want and click its Add button. The product will be added to your basket, where you can view it, change its quantity, or remove it.

Can I save products for later?

Yes. You can use the wishlist feature to save your favourite products and return to them whenever you want.

How can I find a particular product?

Use the search feature or browse by category. You can also sort products and apply price filters to narrow down your choices.

Basket & Checkout

How do I check my basket?

Click the Basket button in the header. Your selected products, quantities and subtotal will appear in the basket dropdown.

Can I change the quantity of a product?

Yes. Open your basket and use the quantity controls beside the product to increase or decrease the number of items.

How do I proceed to checkout?

Add your preferred products to the basket and click Proceed to Checkout. You will be taken to the checkout page to review your selected products and customer details.

Can I remove products from my basket?

Yes. You can remove individual products from your basket or use the Clear Basket option to remove all its contents.

Products from Jumia

Why are some products taking me to Jumia?

Some products displayed on Verdant are affiliate products from Jumia. When you click Buy Now, you are redirected to the original Jumia product page to continue shopping.

Can I purchase Jumia products directly on Verdant?

No. Jumia affiliate products are purchased through Jumia's website. Verdant simply helps you discover those products and directs you to the original store.

Who handles orders for Jumia products?

Orders placed through Jumia are handled by Jumia and the relevant seller. Please refer to Jumia's website for order information and support.

Account & Registration

Do I need an account to use Verdant?

You can explore products without registering. Some account-related features may require you to log in or create an account.

How do I create an account?

Visit the Register page and complete the required information. Follow the instructions on the page to create your account.

What if I cannot log in?

Check that you have entered the correct login details. If the problem continues, you can reach out through our Contact page for assistance.

Delivery

How can I check if delivery is available in my area?

Use the delivery-area checker on our homepage. You can also visit our Delivery Zone page for more information about delivery coverage.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery availability and timing depend on the delivery area and the service arrangements. Check the delivery information provided during the shopping process.
